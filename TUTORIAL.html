1. QUICK START

  • Double-click launcher.bat or Play_Game.vbs to launch in frameless desktop mode.
  • Choose your preferred browser from the menu (Chrome, Edge, Brave, etc.).

2. BATTLEFIELD CONTROLS & HOTKEYS

  • CLICK / TAP Attack toilet invaders & collect energy powerups.
  • [1] Hydraulic Spring Bouncer (15⚡): Launches toilets backward with bowling-pin cascade stuns!
  • [2] Corrosive Acid Puddle (20⚡): Melts enemy armor plates and strips traction.
  • [3] Explosive Sludge Barrel (25⚡): Detonates in a huge AoE blast when shot or clicked.
  • Right-Click Cancel active trap placement mode.
  • SPACEBAR Sigma Blast: Screen-wide EMP pulse when meter is full.
  • [V] TV Hypno-Glow: Freezes all toilets on screen for 6 seconds.
  • [B] Speakerman Bass Drop: Massive sonic knockback shockwave.
  • [H] Alliance Barracks: Recruit Titan Drillman, Mech-Hound, Jetpack Uzi, etc.
  • [S] Weapon Shop: Upgrade passive defense turrets, lasers, rockets.
  • [T] Open / Close In-Game Tutorial.
  • [P] / [ESC] Pause game / Settings.

3. CORE SURVIVAL RULES

  • Defend the yellow striped hazard threshold at the top of the sewer floor. Crossing it drains 1 Life!
  • Defeat Bosses every 5 waves to draft 1 of 3 permanent Sigma Relics.
