[+] QUICK START GUIDE:
1. Double-click 'launcher.bat' (or use 'Play_Game.vbs').

[+] BATTLEFIELD CONTROLS & HOTKEYS:
- Left Click / Tap  : Attack toilets & collect energy powerups
- [1] Key            : Arm Hydraulic Plunger Spring (15⚡ Sigma Energy)
                       -> Launches toilets backward in bowling-pin cascade stuns!
- [2] Key            : Arm Corrosive Acid Puddle (20⚡ Sigma Energy)
                       -> Melts enemy armor plates and saps movement traction!
- [3] Key            : Arm Explosive Bio-Sludge Barrel (25⚡ Sigma Energy)
                       -> Shoot, tap, or ram it to trigger a huge AoE blast!
- Right-Click        : Cancel active trap placement mode
- [SPACEBAR]         : SIGMA BLAST (Screen-wide EMP wipe & Boss damage)
- [V] Key            : TV Man Hypno-Glow (Freezes all active enemies)
- [B] Key            : Speakerman Bass Drop (Heavy radial knockback earthquake)
- [H] Key            : Open Alliance Barracks (Hire Titan Drillman, Mech-Hound, etc.)
- [S] Key            : Open Weapon Shop (Upgrade passive guns, lasers, rockets)
- [P] / [ESC]        : Pause Game / Open Settings Menu

[+] SURVIVAL TACTICS & ARENA RULES:
- Defend the reinforced drainage threshold at the top of the sewer floor!
  Toilets breach when they cross the yellow hazard warning line.
- Ground traps can ONLY be placed on the sewer floor below the breach threshold.
- Down Bosses every 5 waves to trigger the SIGMA RELIC PROTOCOL:
  Draft 1 of 3 permanent game-changing passive artifacts for your run.
